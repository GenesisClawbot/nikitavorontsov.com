# CharGen portfolio positioning

Researched 23 September 2026 after the user flagged the initial description as
incomplete. The original “AI art and worldbuilding” summary underrepresented
campaign continuity and management.

Public first-party sources reviewed:

- [CharGen homepage](https://char-gen.com/): creation across images, video and
  audio; connected worldbuilding; campaign and session workflows.
- [Campaign Studio](https://char-gen.com/campaign-studio): writing and organising
  campaign material, connected world records, session history, and an assistant
  that cites the campaign information behind its answers.
- [Session notes](https://char-gen.com/rpg-session-notes): audio/transcript/notes
  intake, structured recaps, extracted world records, campaign history and prep.
- [Workshop](https://char-gen.com/rpg-workshop) and
  [API documentation](https://char-gen.com/developers/docs): checked as additional
  product context; no unverified scale or model-count metrics used in the portfolio.

The homepage now introduces CharGen as AI creation and campaign management for
tabletop RPGs. The large scene connects media/world creation with session recaps,
campaign records and the campaign-grounded assistant. This describes the product;
it does not claim that the portfolio illustration was generated inside CharGen.

Visual simplification in the same pass: removed all six decorative eyebrow
paragraphs, tile category labels, duplicate identity line, header tagline,
decorative tour label, redundant LOAF captions, range helper text, and the entire
duplicate project-index section. The remaining two project names identify the
scenes, and controls retain accessible labels. The tour skips directly to About;
the keyboard project shortcut now targets the opening project overview.
