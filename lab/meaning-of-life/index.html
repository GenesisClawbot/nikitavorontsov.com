One minute each. Start with any model.

Same question.
Different films.

“What is the point of life? Provide your answer and explanation in a 60-second video that you storyboard, illustrate, and animate yourself.”

Each model chose its own answer, words, imagery and storyboard in a separate iterative tool harness. The runs used xhigh reasoning, actual Blender scenes, HyperFrames assembly and ElevenLabs V4 narration.

I set the brief. Codex handled orchestration, tool repairs and output checks. These are individual creative experiments, rather than a controlled ranking of the models.

The complete films above retain their original sound, speed and duration. The social teaser is a selection of excerpts.
