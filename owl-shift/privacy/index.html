Owl Shift privacy policy

Last updated 4 November 2026

Owl Shift is a diaper tracker and feeding log for Android, made by Nikita Vorontsov in the United Kingdom. This page explains what data the app uses, why, and what you can do about it. You do not need an account to use it, and the app never asks for your name, email address or contacts.

What stays on your phone

Everything you log (diapers, feeds, bottles, notes and your baby's name if you add one) and your settings are saved on your device. They are not sent to us or to anyone else. If Android backup is on for your Google account, Android may include them in your phone's backup, under Google's own terms, so they come back when you change phones. Deleting the app deletes them from the phone.

Children

Owl Shift is not made for children under 18 and is not marketed to them. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has used the app and want their data removed, contact us and we will help.

How long data is kept

Data on your phone stays until you delete the app. The services above keep data for the periods set out in their own policies: RevenueCat.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask to see the personal data held about you, have it corrected or deleted, object to its use, or restrict it. Because the app does not ask who you are, the simplest way to remove your data is to delete the app. For anything else, write to us at contact@char-gen.com. If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Deleting your data

Owl Shift does not ask who you are, so most of your data never leaves your phone. To delete what there is:

  • Your log and settings are on your phone. Uninstalling Owl Shift deletes them.
  • The "Owl Shift Plus" purchase is kept by Google Play and RevenueCat as a purchase record, as the law requires for transactions. You can ask us to delete the RevenueCat record by email; the purchase itself then cannot be restored.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Significant changes will also be shown in the app.
