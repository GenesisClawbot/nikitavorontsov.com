a brief guide to loafing

One button.
Many cats.

  1. Wait for the floating cat to line up.
  2. Tap, click, or press Space to drop it.
  3. Keep every cat on the cushion. A falling cat ends your run.

A centred landing earns a purrfect. Every challenge link uses the same cat sequence. Your best score stays on this device.

After a fall, Keep building lets you carry on with the cats that remain. Free build keeps your original best score safe. The speaker controls the quiet background music and meows.
