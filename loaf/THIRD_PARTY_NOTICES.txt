## Cat recording

audio/gentle-meow-v1.mp3:
"Meow (Gentle)" by sokworks, a recording of Sugar the cat.
https://freesound.org/people/sokworks/sounds/866536/
Creative Commons CC0 1.0 Universal Public Domain Dedication:
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

The recording's official high-quality MP3 preview was converted to mono
44.1 kHz MP3 and level-adjusted for the game. Its full vocal envelope and
natural pitch are retained. The user selected this recording after audition.

## Background music

audio/loaf-groove-v1.mp3:
An original instrumental generated for LOAF with ElevenLabs Music v2.5
through WaveSpeed on 20 September 2026, prediction
61fa1d0369fd4365bc434d0a71ffbf01. The brief requested a lightly swung,
96 BPM acoustic groove with plucked bass, felt piano and brushed percussion.
The file was level-adjusted, trimmed to 40 seconds, given 20 ms edge fades
and encoded as 128 kbps stereo MP3. No reference song or artist was supplied.

Playback is local; no generation service is called by the game.
