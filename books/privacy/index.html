Book Companion privacy policy

Last updated 19 October 2026

CharGen operates Book Companion. Contact us about privacy at contact@char-gen.com. This policy covers the app and the support pages under nikitavorontsov.com/books/.

Your shelf and account

In guest mode, your book titles and authors, chapter progress, ratings, saved reminders, reading sessions, profile name and mood preferences are stored on your device. Looking up a reference sends your selected reference edition and reading boundary, the lookup or selected entry, and ordinary network metadata to our service. Guest mode does not mean all features work offline.

If you create an account, Supabase handles your email address, password authentication and session. Your shelf, profile, bookmarks and reading sessions are synced to your private cloud library. Account records also include an account identifier and operational timestamps. Your password is not included in that library or in an AI request.

We use these records to provide the account, syncing and reading features you request. Providing an email address is necessary for an account; you can use guest tracking without one. Your library and ratings are not published as a public profile.

Questions and OpenAI

Before your first question, the app asks whether you want to allow AI questions. When allowed, our server sends OpenAI your current question, up to four earlier questions from the current conversation with references to their answers, and the checked book information available at your reading point. An earlier question that names someone may be kept to understand follow-ups. The request does not automatically include your email, profile or reading-session history. Information you type into a question is still part of the question, so avoid personal or sensitive details.

OpenAI selects from checked reference statements. We request that the response is not stored as a saved API response, and do not create a persistent AI conversation. The current app keeps the open conversation in memory rather than in your synced library.

OpenAI’s standard abuse-monitoring logs may contain questions and responses and are kept for up to 30 days, with legal or safety exceptions. Prompt caching may retain application state for up to 24 hours. These are separate from a saved conversation; the app does not promise zero provider retention. See OpenAI’s data controls.

You can withdraw your permission in You → Questions & privacy. This stops new questions and clears the open conversation. It does not recall a request already sent. Name lookup and guest tracking remain available. We use your permission for this optional feature; account and sync processing provides the service you request.

Service providers and support

  • Supabase provides account authentication, the private library database and the app’s server endpoints. Requests involve network and security metadata such as IP addresses. See Supabase’s privacy information.
  • Amazon Web Services SES delivers confirmation and recovery emails, including the recipient address, message and delivery metadata. See AWS privacy information.
  • OpenAI processes optional questions as described above.
  • Support email contains your address, message and any attachments you choose to send. We use it to handle your request. Please do not send passwords or authentication links.

We use service and security records to operate the app, prevent misuse and investigate faults. These operational purposes are our legitimate interests. We may also retain records where legally required. Providers may process information outside your country under their applicable data-processing terms and transfer safeguards. Contact us for information about the arrangements relevant to your request.

Retention and deletion

Your live account and synced library remain until you delete the account. Signing out does not delete saved account drafts on your device. An account deletion removes its live authentication account and synced library; successful in-app cleanup also removes that account’s draft and question permission on the device used for deletion.

Your separate guest shelf stays. Other devices may retain their own local copies, and provider security logs and backups may remain under their retention rules or legal obligations. A minimal deleted-account identifier stays on the deleting device to prevent stale data from being restored. It is not an anonymous record.

If online deletion cannot be confirmed, the app lets you explicitly choose to remove that account’s local copy, including unsynced changes, while preserving guest books and other accounts. This does not confirm online deletion. The device retains the account identifier to block restoration and record the unresolved online request. These minimal records have no automatic expiry and do not include the removed library or credentials. Contact support to resolve the online request; doing so does not automatically clear the device records.

Reference information may be cached on your device. You can clear site/app storage through your device or browser settings where supported; backup and secure-storage behaviour varies by platform. We do not promise that signing out or uninstalling immediately erases every copy.

Support correspondence is kept while handling the request and as needed for follow-up or legal obligations. Provider logs and backup copies have separate retention; a live account deletion is not a promise of immediate deletion from every provider system. You can ask us about retained information and request its removal where applicable.

Visits to these support pages

GitHub Pages hosts this website and receives ordinary access and security information. The site’s shared Plausible analytics counts visits. This website analytics is separate from your reading activity in the app. These pages do not contain an account sign-in form. See GitHub’s privacy statement and Plausible’s data policy.

Your choices and rights

Depending on the law that applies to you, you may ask to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your personal data, restrict its use, or object to processing based on legitimate interests. You can withdraw permission for optional AI questions without changing the lawfulness of earlier processing. Email contact@char-gen.com. We may need to verify account ownership before acting.

If you are concerned that a child has shared personal information through the app, contact us. If you are unhappy with how we handle your information, you can contact your local data-protection regulator; in the UK this is the Information Commissioner’s Office.

Changes to this policy

We will update this page and its date when how the app handles data changes. Check this page before using newly introduced features.
