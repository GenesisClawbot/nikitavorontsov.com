PageOtter support

A reading companion from CharGen · Currently in testing

Keep your place, remember a character and track your reading. This page is for help with the PageOtter app. A book on your shelf may not yet have reference or question coverage.

Email support

Contact contact@char-gen.com. Tell us your device, app version and what went wrong. For a reference problem, include the book, edition and last chapter you have finished. Please do not send passwords, sign-in links or sensitive personal information.

Accounts and email links

You can use the app as a guest. An account adds syncing for your shelf, reading activity and profile. Open confirmation and password-reset links on the same device where you requested them, with the app installed. For a password reset, sign out first, then request a fresh link from the account sheet.

If a link has expired or opens the wrong place, return to the app and request a new one. Check your spam folder and allow a little time before requesting another email. Help with an email link.

Questions and your reading point

Set your progress to the chapters you have finished. Quick name lookup uses the available reference. Questions need an internet connection and your permission to send the question and checked context to OpenAI.

The current pilot supports a small checked reference and selected character-recall and connection questions. It does not cover complete books or every title you can add. An unavailable answer does not tell you whether a fact exists later in the story.

Reading activity

Log pages and minutes in Activity to see your weekly reading and pages per hour. Update your chapter separately. Mood choices in your profile describe your reading preferences.

Your data

Read how your data is used, or request account and data deletion. You do not need to reinstall the app to make a deletion request.
