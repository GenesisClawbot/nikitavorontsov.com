Delete your Book Companion account

Book Companion · Operated by CharGen

You can request deletion without reinstalling or signing into the app.

Request account deletion by email

Email contact@char-gen.com with the subject Book Companion account deletion, ideally from the address used for the account. If you cannot access that address, say so and we will explain how to verify ownership. Do not send your password or a sign-in link.

If the button does not open an email app, copy the address above into your email service. Opening a draft does not submit the request: you need to send the email. We verify ownership before deleting account data and tell you when the request has been completed. We aim to respond within one month; if verification or a lawful exception affects the request, we will explain.

Delete from inside the app

  1. Open You, then your account.
  2. Choose Delete account.
  3. Read the confirmation and confirm deletion.

If the app reports that the account was deleted online but device cleanup is unfinished, keep the app open and use the cleanup retry. Contact support if it cannot finish.

If online deletion cannot be confirmed

Open Recovery options and choose Continue on this device to review local removal. You must explicitly confirm before the app clears that account’s local copy, including unsynced changes. Your guest books and other accounts stay saved. If local cleanup is interrupted, use Retry local cleanup; this does not send another online deletion request.

Local copy removed does not mean the online account was deleted. The app keeps a reminder that online deletion still needs checking, and that account stays unavailable on the device. Email support to finish checking the online request. If the saved request is damaged and the app cannot identify its account safely, it offers support instead of local removal.

What is removed

Your live login account and synced library are removed. This includes the account’s saved books, chapter progress, ratings, bookmarks, reading sessions, profile name and mood preferences. Successful in-app cleanup also clears that account’s local draft and question permission on that device.

What may remain

  • Your separate guest shelf is preserved.
  • Other devices can retain local account copies. We cannot promise remote erasure from an offline device.
  • A minimal account identifier remains on the deleting device to prevent stale restoration. After local-only removal, a separate record identifies the unresolved online request. Neither has an automatic expiry.
  • Provider security logs, backups and records required by law have separate retention. Deletion does not recall questions already sent to OpenAI or automatically erase support correspondence.

You can also email us to request deletion of particular personal data without closing your account. Describe the data you want removed; we will explain any limits. See the privacy policy for provider retention and your choices.
